Learn Personal Branding with Phoebe

Before anyone reads what you say about yourself, they search your name, and the first page that comes back is the version of you they meet. Personal branding, done honestly, is the slow work of making that page show your actual work: a niche only you can claim, proof instead of adjectives, a cadence you can keep, and ground you own. Six sessions, one constructed running case, and one piece of advice everyone repeats, post more, tested on 302 real accounts and 22,657 public answers counted in your browser. Across creators it looks like a law. Against their own quiet weeks, it buys volume and no growth.

The six sessions 🎯 · from what a stranger finds to a system you keep

The running case is Ines Calloway, a constructed mid-career data analyst who forecasts demand for a regional grocery chain and wants the right people to find her. She is invented for teaching, and every page says so. Alongside her, every build-along runs on your own name and your own work.

🔎 Session 1 · foundations

A reputation is a search result

Before anyone reads your profile, they search your name. Ten results, four kinds, two honest counts, and the one result you will add first. Build-along: the audit on your own name.

▶ Start here 🧭 Session 2 · core

Your niche: the onliness test

Positioning for one person: the line about your work nobody else could sign. The competitor swap, Kevin Kelly's arithmetic for a small niche, and a measured checker for your line.

audience, outcome, proof 🛠️ Session 3 · core

Voice and proof: show the work

Proof over claims. Austin Kleon's ten rules for showing process, a ladder from claim to artefact someone else used, and voice as a set of choices. Build-along: one real piece of work, made public.

the proof ladder 🎛️ Session 4 · bench night

The consistency bench

Does posting more grow engagement? 302 real accounts and 22,657 answers counted in your browser: the cross-creator chart, the survivor trap, the same creator against themselves, and a break button.

★ the bench 🏡 Session 5 · deeper

Platforms and formats: owned and rented ground

Where your readers actually read, the audience you own against the one you rent, digital sharecropping, and choosing a format for the reader rather than the platform's fashion.

owned vs rented 🌱 Session 6 · deeper

A system you keep

A cadence you can sustain, an idea bank that refills itself, and a quarterly re-run of the session 1 audit so the before and after are evidence. Final scorecard.
